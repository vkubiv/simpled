Fixes for the controller from a review of 2.1.0 and 2.1.1. Nothing changes in the
env spec or the generated deployment; redeploying picks the new controller up.

## Certificates on Docker Swarm

- **A failed order is retried sooner.** After 2, 4, 8, 16 and 32 minutes, then hourly,
  doubling up to a day. Right after a deploy the gateway may not route the challenges
  yet; before, a failed first order cost at least an hour on the self-signed
  placeholder. A retry that comes due while other domains are being tried runs at
  once. A CA that cannot be reached for the account is retried on the same ladder.
- **No gateway update, and no pruning of old certificate secrets, while a deploy
  holds the swarm's deploy lock.** The update used to race `docker stack deploy`
  and could abort the deploy with `update out of sequence`. The lock is read right
  before the update, only when the gateway has to move; a lock older than 30 minutes,
  or without the time it was taken, is one a crashed deploy left and does not count.
- A certificate past its expiry reports `tls: expired`, a new, worst state; it was
  `expiring`.
- When the account cannot be obtained, the gateway is still moved onto certificates
  the other domains already have.

## Health and the private API

- **Route-less deployments** (workers only) are listed: `/simpled/health/<workers>`
  answers `down` until its services run, instead of 404, so `simpled wait` on them
  works.
- **Failed tokens behind a shared address.** Ten failed attempts from an address get
  429 until a minute has passed without a failure. Past the tenth, one request from
  that address at a time is answered, after a growing delay, and the others are
  refused at once with their token unread. A valid token from a shared proxy
  address is refused only while another request from there is waiting, and is no
  longer locked out for a minute by someone else's failures.
- `/simpled/health` and `/simpled/api/v1/status` read every deployment from one
  listing, and concurrent reads of the certificates share one listing.
- A controller token fetched from AWS is trimmed and checked against the 32-character
  minimum by the fetch script, before anything is written; the controller used to
  refuse it quietly and the API did not exist.
- On standalone Docker, a labelled container without a deployment label is no longer
  reported as a deployment named `""`.

## `simpled wait`

- Fails once the controller has answered `no such deployment` three polls in a row,
  with the controller's message; a single such answer, as from a controller task that
  predates a freshly added deployment, is waited out.
- The gateway's own 404, before it routes the controller right after a deploy, is
  waited out as before.
- Other answers that are not the health document are reported with their status code
  instead of "unknown at no version".
